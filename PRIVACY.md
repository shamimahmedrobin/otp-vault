# Privacy Policy — OTP Vault

_Last updated: 19 November 2026_

OTP Vault is designed so that your data never leaves your device.

## What the app stores

- OTP codes, optional phone numbers and optional notes **you type in manually**, stored locally in an encrypted database (AES-256-GCM, Android Keystore).
- A salted, stretched verifier of your app PIN (never the PIN itself).
- App preferences (theme, language, lock settings).

## What the app never does

- No internet permission — nothing is uploaded anywhere.
- No SMS reading, no notification interception, no accessibility tricks.
- No accounts, no cloud backend, no Firebase/Supabase.
- No advertising SDKs, no analytics, no crash reporting that could contain OTP data.
- No connection to bKash, Nagad, banks or any financial service.

## Backups

Encrypted backups are created only when you explicitly choose a destination file and a backup password. They are never uploaded automatically. The backup file contains sensitive data — store it somewhere safe.

## Data deletion

Expired OTPs are deleted automatically. You can delete individual entries, clear all entries, or wipe all local data (including the PIN verifier and encryption key) from Settings at any time.
